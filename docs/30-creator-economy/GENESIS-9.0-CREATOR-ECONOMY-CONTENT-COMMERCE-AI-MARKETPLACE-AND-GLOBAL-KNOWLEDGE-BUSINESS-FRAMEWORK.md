# Genesis 9.0 — Creator Economy, Content Commerce, AI Marketplace & Global Knowledge Business Framework

**Theme:** Build the commercial knowledge economy of LearningOS.

## Vision

Enable educators, experts, institutions, creators, developers, and enterprises to transform knowledge into governed, licensable, measurable, and globally distributable products and services.

## Objectives

- Provide creator onboarding, storefronts, catalogs, and business management.
- Support sales, subscriptions, bundles, licensing, royalties, and enterprise procurement.
- Establish marketplaces for content, credentials, services, plugins, datasets, and AI assets.
- Protect ownership, permissions, usage rights, and revenue attribution.
- Deliver transparent commerce analytics, settlements, and governance.

## Core Modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Creator Business Center | Manages identity, brand, catalog, and operations |
| Knowledge Marketplace | Distributes courses, books, media, and services |
| AI Marketplace | Distributes agents, prompts, models, workflows, and tools |
| Licensing & Rights Engine | Governs ownership and authorized use |
| Commerce & Checkout | Supports purchases, subscriptions, and invoicing |
| Royalty & Payout Engine | Calculates splits, royalties, taxes, and settlement |
| Enterprise Procurement | Supports institutional licensing and contracts |
| Commerce Intelligence | Measures revenue, demand, conversion, and retention |

## Commerce Lifecycle

```text
Create → Verify Rights → Package → Price → Publish → Sell/License → Deliver → Settle → Improve
```

## Major Capabilities

Creators can operate branded storefronts, manage product variants, bundles, memberships, promotions, affiliates, customer access, support, analytics, and tax-ready records. Products may include learning programs, credentials, books, templates, assessments, coaching, media, datasets, software components, AI agents, and automation packs.

The rights engine records authorship, ownership, contributors, permitted territories, duration, seat limits, derivative rights, attribution, resale, sublicensing, and expiration. Revenue rules support platform fees, collaborators, institutions, affiliates, publishers, and regional tax obligations.

The AI marketplace requires security review, capability descriptions, data-use disclosures, permission scopes, versioning, performance monitoring, and responsible-AI policies.
