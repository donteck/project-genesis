# Project Genesis Discovery Registry

Discoveries capture moments where our understanding fundamentally changed.

Unlike standards or blueprints, discoveries are recognized after they occur. They preserve the reasoning behind a change in direction and record insights that permanently reshape the mission, architecture, or ecosystem of Project Genesis.

A Discovery is not:

- A blueprint
- A constitution
- A standard
- A technical specification

Each Discovery receives:

- Sequential number
- Title
- Date
- Summary
- Impact
- Related discoveries
- Related constitutions
- Related blueprints

## Permanence

Discoveries are permanent historical records. They should remain recoverable, reviewable, and connected to the constitutions and blueprints they influenced so future contributors can understand not only what changed, but why.

Discovery 005 is the capstone of the first Project Genesis Discovery series.

Discovery 006 establishes the multiplication principle through which the knowledge preserved by that series can reach people across media, products, learning experiences, and generations.

Discovery 007 establishes the four-level learning model through which those expressions guide people from Information to Knowledge to Principles to Wisdom.

Discovery 008 establishes the Builder's Principle underlying the governing duties of the Constitution of Builders.

Discovery 013 establishes the Legacy Principle: Project Genesis must preserve enough knowledge, context, mentorship, and institutional continuity for value to continue beyond its original creators.
