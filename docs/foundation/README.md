# Project Genesis Foundation

The Foundation contains the highest-level governing documents of Project Genesis. These documents define the enduring purpose, beliefs, identity, commitments, and school of thought from which governance, architecture, standards, and implementation descend.

The Foundation is distinct from the numbered [Foundations Registry](../foundations/README.md), which preserves individual timeless principles. This directory is the governing-document home above those records.

## Foundational Documents

- [The Genesis Declaration](THE_GENESIS_DECLARATION.md) — establishes Project Genesis as an Engineering School of Thought centered on purposeful software architecture, knowledge stewardship, AI-native engineering, and lifelong learning.

Future documents may be referenced here only when their authority and relationship to the governing hierarchy have been explicitly established.

## Recommended Hierarchy

```text
Vision
  ↓
Manifesto
  ↓
Genesis Declaration
  ↓
Charter
  ↓
Constitutions
  ↓
Canonical Blueprints
  ↓
Discoveries
  ↓
Engineering Standards
  ↓
Reference Architectures
  ↓
Implementation Guides
```

Each level translates the authority above it into greater specificity. Lower levels may implement, interpret, and extend higher-level direction, but they may not silently contradict it.

## Stewardship

Foundational governing documents change only through deliberate, transparent review proportionate to their authority. Historical versions, reasons for change, and relationships to dependent documents must remain preserved.
