# Volume 10 — The Historical Archives

## Purpose

The Historical Archives preserve knowledge that is no longer current but remains necessary for understanding how Project Genesis evolved.

## Scope

This volume covers:

- Legacy documents
- Retired Blueprints
- Historical versions
- Deprecated standards
- Archived releases

Archived material should retain provenance, dates, prior status, reason for retirement, successor references, and any continuing constraints required for safe interpretation.

## Future Contents

Future contents may include immutable historical editions, migration records, retired architecture, superseded policies, release archives, legacy interfaces, deprecation notices, restoration instructions, and curated retrospectives.

## Relationship to Other Volumes

The Historical Archives receive material superseded by every active volume without erasing its contribution. The Evolution Journal explains the transition into history, while active volumes link to archived predecessors when that history is necessary to understand current knowledge.
