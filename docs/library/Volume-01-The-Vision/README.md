# Volume 01 — The Vision

## Purpose

The Vision preserves the purpose, mission, and identity of Project Genesis. It defines the future Project Genesis exists to help create and the enduring human need that gives the work direction.

## Scope

This volume covers foundational statements of purpose, mission, identity, long-term vision, parent-organization context, and the relationship between Project Genesis, Merchay Global Vision, LearningOS, and future initiatives.

## Future Contents

Future contents may include the canonical Project Genesis Vision, mission records, identity statements, long-horizon narratives, vision amendments, and approved interpretations that clarify the intended future without reducing it to a temporary roadmap.

## Relationship to Other Volumes

The Vision gives every other volume direction. Volume 02 explains the philosophy used to pursue it; Volume 03 declares it; Volume 04 translates it into blueprints; Volumes 06 and 07 make it operational; Volume 08 demonstrates it through LearningOS; and Volumes 09 and 10 preserve how its expression changes through time.
