# Volume 03 — The Manifesto

## Purpose

The Manifesto preserves the philosophical declaration of Project Genesis: the beliefs, rejections, commitments, and promises that every generation of builders is invited to carry forward.

## Scope

This volume is reserved for the Project Genesis Manifesto, its authoritative context, and any future revisions approved under extraordinary circumstances. The Manifesto is not a Blueprint, Discovery, Constitution, standard, or ordinary documentation artifact.

## Future Contents

Future contents may include the canonical Manifesto, signed or published editions, translations, version history, amendment records, and explanatory notes required to preserve the meaning of an extraordinary revision.

## Relationship to Other Volumes

The Manifesto declares the Vision and Philosophy preserved in Volumes 01 and 02. The Canonical Blueprints and Engineering Standards should express its commitments in actionable form, while the Evolution Journal and Historical Archives preserve its continuity and any future change.
