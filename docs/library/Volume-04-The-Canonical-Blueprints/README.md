# Volume 04 — The Canonical Blueprints

## Purpose

The Canonical Blueprints volume curates all official Project Genesis Blueprints as an ordered body of authoritative design and institutional knowledge.

## Scope

This volume covers every document formally designated as a Canonical Blueprint, including its number, title, status, purpose, relationships, amendments, and place within the wider Project Genesis architecture.

## Future Contents

Future contents may include curated Blueprint editions, volume indexes, thematic cross-references, dependency maps, amendment records, and reading paths that connect philosophical, product, educational, organizational, and engineering Blueprints.

## Relationship to Other Volumes

The Blueprints translate the Vision, Philosophy, and Manifesto into durable models and implications. They inform Engineering Standards and Architecture, are demonstrated in the LearningOS Case Study, respond to Discoveries, and leave their revision history to the Evolution Journal and Historical Archives.
