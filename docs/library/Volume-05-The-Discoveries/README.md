# Volume 05 — The Discoveries

## Purpose

The Discoveries volume preserves the realizations that materially changed how Project Genesis understands its mission, philosophy, architecture, products, or responsibilities.

## Scope

This volume covers all official Discovery documents, their originating context, evidence, implications, relationships, and influence on later Blueprints, standards, decisions, and product evolution.

## Future Contents

Future contents may include curated Discovery editions, chronological and thematic indexes, relationship maps, retrospective commentary, and records showing how a discovery changed subsequent work.

## Relationship to Other Volumes

Discoveries deepen the Vision and Philosophy and may motivate new or revised Canonical Blueprints, Engineering Standards, and Architecture. Their practical expression can be examined in the LearningOS Case Study, while their sequence belongs to the Evolution Journal and superseded interpretations belong in the Historical Archives.
