# Genesis 9.3 — Marketing Automation, CRM, Growth Platform, Community Building & Audience Intelligence Framework

**Theme:** Establish the relationship, demand-generation, and community-growth layer of LearningOS.

## Vision

Create a unified system that understands prospects, learners, customers, creators, institutions, and partners across their complete relationship lifecycle and uses governed automation to deliver timely, relevant, and measurable engagement.

## Objectives

- Build a unified CRM for individuals and organizations.
- Automate lead capture, qualification, nurturing, conversion, and retention.
- Support customer success, community, referrals, and advocacy.
- Personalize journeys using consented behavioral and profile intelligence.
- Connect marketing performance to enrollment, revenue, learning, and lifetime value.

## Core Modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Unified CRM | Maintains people, organizations, relationships, and activity |
| Marketing Automation | Executes campaigns and lifecycle journeys |
| Lead & Opportunity Management | Tracks qualification, pipeline, and conversion |
| Customer Success Platform | Manages onboarding, health, retention, and expansion |
| Community Platform | Supports groups, discussions, events, and peer learning |
| Referral & Affiliate Engine | Tracks invitations, attribution, rewards, and advocacy |
| Personalization Engine | Selects relevant messages, offers, and experiences |
| Audience Intelligence | Measures segments, intent, engagement, and value |

## Major Capabilities

The CRM maintains profiles, organizations, contacts, roles, preferences, consent, communications, products, enrollments, transactions, support history, opportunities, and engagement timelines.

Automation supports triggers, segments, branching, scheduling, scoring, approvals, channel preferences, frequency controls, experiments, attribution, and human handoff. High-impact or sensitive communications remain governed.

Customer success includes onboarding plans, health scores, milestones, usage signals, risks, tasks, playbooks, renewals, expansion opportunities, and intervention workflows.

Community capabilities include public and private groups, cohort spaces, discussion, events, moderation, expert sessions, recognition, member directories, and knowledge sharing.

Audience intelligence connects acquisition source, content engagement, learning behavior, commerce, community activity, satisfaction, retention, and lifetime value.
