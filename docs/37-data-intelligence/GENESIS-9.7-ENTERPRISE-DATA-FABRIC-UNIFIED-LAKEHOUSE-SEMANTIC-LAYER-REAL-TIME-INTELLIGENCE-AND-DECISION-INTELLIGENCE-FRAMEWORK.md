**Theme:** Build the trusted data intelligence foundation of LearningOS.

## Vision

- Build the Enterprise Data Fabric.
- Create a Unified Lakehouse Architecture.

## Major Platform Capabilities

The Data Fabric connects databases, event streams, APIs, files, documents, learning systems, financial systems, AI memory, marketplace activity, and approved external datasets without forcing every source into one physical store.

The Lakehouse supports structured, semi-structured, document, media-metadata, event, research, and AI-interaction data for historical analysis, machine learning, reporting, and governed exploration.

The catalog records descriptions, technical metadata, classification, owners, quality, lineage, access policy, usage guidance, and versions. The semantic layer creates consistent definitions for learners, institutions, courses, credentials, products, skills, revenue, and research.

Master Data Management resolves duplicates and authoritative records. Real-time intelligence detects operational conditions from learning, finance, AI, security, marketplace, and engagement events.

Decision intelligence combines governed data, models, scenarios, recommendations, explanations, and human judgment. Digital twins support safe simulation of institutional, learner, financial, and infrastructure scenarios.
